Stream or download audiobooks hosted on your Plex server with Chronicle, the loved Audiobook Player for Plex — now built for Wear OS, so your books go wherever your watch does.

<b>📚 Key Features</b>

• <b>Sync Progress</b> - Keep your audiobook progress synchronized across your devices
• <b>Multiple Formats</b> - Support for mp3, m4a, and m4b audiobook files
• <b>Adjustable Playback Speed</b> - Listen at your own pace with customizable playback speeds
• <b>Auto-Rewind</b> - Never lose your place with automatic rewind on resume
• <b>Sleep Timer</b> - Fall asleep to your favorite books with built-in sleep timer
• <b>Skip Silence</b> - Automatically skip silent portions for efficient listening
• <b>Download Books</b> - Download audiobooks to your watch for offline playback anytime, anywhere

<b>⌚ Built for the Wrist</b>

Chronicle is designed specifically for Plex users who want a dedicated audiobook player on their wrist, with a Wear OS interface built for quick glances and rotary-crown navigation. Connect to your Plex server and enjoy your entire audiobook collection without needing your phone in hand.

<b>🎯 Requirements</b>

• A Plex server with audiobook content
• A Wear OS watch
• Network access to your Plex server
